### State Key Laboratory of Respiratory Disease, Guangzhou Medical University
**Research Collaborator**, Thoracic Surgery Department and Thoracic Oncology Group · *Feb 2024 – Present*

- *Achievements:* one paper accepted at ACM Multimedia 2026.
