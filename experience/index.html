Guangzhou National Laboratory

Research Engineer (May – Oct 2026) · Research Collaborator (Feb 2024 – Apr 2026)

Member of Prof. Jianxing He's group, a joint team of Guangzhou National Laboratory and the State Key Laboratory of Respiratory Disease, Guangzhou Medical University. I joined as a research collaborator during my undergraduate studies and worked full-time as a Research Engineer after graduating.

  • AI sandbox hospital: core developer of a simulated hospital in which LLM agents take on clinical roles; under review at ACL ARR.
  • Medical reasoning chains: wrote the group's specification for medical chain-of-thought — how EHR reasoning is decomposed into steps, how clinical visual evidence is cited, and the principles for RLHF annotation — used as the common reference for multimodal data construction and model evaluation.
  • Reasoning evaluation: designed the reasoning-chain evaluation framework — dimensions, scoring rules and spot-check protocol — used to compare models and data versions across iterations, and turned it into an annotation protocol for a team of medical students.
  • Process reward modelling (BioMedPRM): designed a process reward model that scores each step of a biomedical reasoning chain rather than only its final answer and transfers across tasks, with emphasis on robustness under distribution shift and in out-of-distribution clinical settings.
  • Source-free domain adaptation: found that weakly supervised segmentation of lung-cancer histopathology loses region-of-interest activation when transferred across centres with different staining protocols; designed a diffusion-based source-free adaptation pipeline that restores high-accuracy segmentation without source data or extra annotation.
  • Training-free multimodal LLM: built a training-free framework for CT and whole-slide images, adapting long-context models to high-dimensional medical imaging without task-specific fine-tuning.
  • Output: first-authored papers at ACM Multimedia 2026, IEEE ICME 2026 (oral) and ICIC 2025 (oral); one submission under review at ACL ARR.
